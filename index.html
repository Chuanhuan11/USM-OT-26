Frequently Asked Questions

Who can participate in the tournament?+
The tournament is open to all players. Categories are available for Open, Ladies, IPTA/IPTS (university students), U18, U15 (individual only), and U12. Age is determined as of 1st January 2026. A younger player may register for a higher-age group but not vice versa.
What are the tournament rules and time control?+
The tournament follows FIDE rules. Time control is 15 minutes + 5 seconds increment per move for each player. Players must report attendance before 8:20AM. Players absent within 15 minutes of game start lose by default.
Are mobile phones allowed in the playing hall?+
No. Mobile phones and any electronic communication devices are strictly prohibited in the playing hall. Violation results in automatic loss of the game.
What is the dress code?+
Smart casual wear is required — collar shirts or T-shirts, jeans or skirts (for ladies), and shoes. Skirts above the knee and slippers are NOT allowed.
What is the refund policy?+
Refund requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks before the event. Approved refunds will be processed and received at least 3 months after the event. Wrong payments will result in cancellation of registration.
Is walk-in registration allowed?+
No. Walk-in registration is strictly NOT permitted. All participants must register online before the closing date.
What is the PCA discount?+
PCA refers to the Penang Chess Association. Only members of PCA for year 2026 are eligible for the discounted PCA rate. Malaysian nationals who are not PCA members are considered Non-PCA. Non-Malaysian nationals are considered International participants.
How many rounds are in the Individual tournament?+
The Individual tournament uses the Swiss System with 8 rounds (subject to number of participants). Pairings are generated via the Swiss Manager Program.
What are the tiebreak criteria for Individual?+
Tiebreaks are resolved in this order: (1) Direct Encounter, (2) Buchholz Tie-Break Variable 2023 (0,0,1,-,-,0), (3) Buchholz Tie-Break Variable 2023 (0,0,0,-,-,0), (4) Sonneborn-Berger, (5) FIDE Tiebreak (Progressive Score).
Can a player win prizes in multiple categories?+
No. Each player may win only ONE prize. If a player qualifies for prizes in more than one category, the hierarchy (Open > Ladies > IPTA/IPTS > U18 > U15 > U12) applies.
What are the categories for Individual?+
Categories: Open, Ladies, IPTA/IPTS, U18 Boys (U18B), U18 Girls (U18G), U15 Girls (U15G), U12 Boys (U12B), U12 Girls (U12G). Cross-category pairings are possible during play.
How many players form a team?+
Each team must consist of exactly 4 players. No reserve players are allowed. Players must be registered in board order upon submission and board order cannot be changed after registration.
How many rounds are in the Team tournament?+
The Team tournament uses the Swiss System with 7 rounds. Pairings are generated via the Swiss Manager Program and cross-age/cross-category pairings are possible.
How is team category determined if members are from different categories?+
If team members belong to different categories, the team's category is determined by the member with the highest priority category, following this order: Open > Ladies > IPTA/IPTS > U18 > U12.
What are the tiebreak criteria for Team?+
Tiebreaks: (1) Match Point (2 for Win, 1 for Draw, 0 for Loss), (2) Game Points, (3) Buchholz Tie-Break Variable 2023 (0,0,0,-,-,0).
What happens if the whole team is absent?+
Teams may be disqualified if the entire team is absent for 2 consecutive rounds. Individual players absent within 15 minutes of game start lose by default (walkover).
